Earth textures bundled from the official Three.js examples:

Local file: earth-atmos-2048.jpg
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg
2048 x 1024 Earth daytime surface map.

Local file: earth-lights-2048.png
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/textures/planets/earth_lights_2048.png
2048 x 1024 nighttime Earth image with real city-light distributions.

Local file: earth-clouds-1024.png
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/textures/planets/earth_clouds_1024.png
1024 x 512 separate cloud coverage image with an embedded transparency table.
Browser decoding preserves its alpha channel (0-255); use that cloud alpha.
